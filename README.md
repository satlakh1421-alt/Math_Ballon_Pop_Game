# 🎈 Math Balloon Pop Game

An engaging and interactive educational game that combines fun balloon-popping action with math practice! Test your reflexes and mental math skills as you pop the right balloons to solve equations.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Action-Packed Learning:** Combine fast-paced arcade gameplay with mathematical problem-solving.
- **Dynamic Challenges:** Pop balloons containing the correct answers to equations before time runs out.
- **Score Tracking:** Keep track of your correct answers and aim for a high score.
- **Lightweight & Responsive:** Designed for smooth performance.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Start a new game session.
2. Look at the math question or target equation displayed on the screen.
3. Click, tap, or shoot the balloon that displays the correct answer.
4. Avoid incorrect answers or penalties to keep your streak going!
